Leleka

XMPP messenger client for Android

Leleka is an open-source XMPP client for Android, based on Conversations. It connects to any XMPP server of your choice and can wake up by push notifications through the push gateway of your own server, so it does not have to keep a permanent connection.

Leleka (Лелека) is Ukrainian for stork — a bird that builds its nest and comes back to it.
