Privacy policy

Leleka for Android (app.leleka.chat) · Effective October 12, 2026

Українською Home

In short: Leleka is a client. It talks to the XMPP server you choose, and — only if you set one up — to a push gateway through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. The developer of Leleka does not run any server that receives your data, and the app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who is responsible

Leleka is developed by fejdraus as an open-source fork of Conversations (GNU GPL v3). Contact: github.com/fejdraus/leleka-site/issues.

Your XMPP server and, if you use one, your push gateway are run by their operators (you, your organisation or your provider). How they handle data is governed by their own policies.

Data the app processes and where it goes

DataPurpose and recipient
XMPP address and password Stored on your device and sent only to your XMPP server to sign in. The connection is always encrypted (TLS), and the password is sent only after the server certificate has been verified.
Messages, files, contact list, profile, presence Exchanged with your XMPP server and the servers of the people you talk to, as XMPP requires. Messages can be end-to-end encrypted with OMEMO. Stored on your device and on the servers according to their settings.
Audio and video calls Sent directly between the devices, or through STUN/TURN servers provided by your XMPP server. Calls are encrypted (DTLS-SRTP).
Push notifications (optional, per account) Only if you enter a push gateway for an account. The app then gets a Firebase Cloud Messaging token from Google Play services for the Firebase project you entered, and sends it together with an app-specific device identifier (Android ID) to your XMPP server, which passes it to the push gateway. The gateway wakes the app up through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. These wake-up messages do not contain message content. Google processes them under the Google Privacy Policy. Without a push gateway the app keeps a permanent connection to your server instead.
Location Only when you choose to share or view a location. The location is sent as a message to the chosen contact. Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers, which see your IP address (OSMF Privacy Policy).
Install referrer When the app is installed from an invitation link, the Google Play install referrer is read on the device to finish the invitation. It is not sent to the developer.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or profiling.
  • No crash reports or usage statistics are sent to the developer.
  • The address book of the phone is not read or uploaded.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties by the developer.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept on your device until you delete it. Removing an account in the app deletes its data from the device. To delete the account itself and the messages stored on the server, use the account deletion function of your XMPP server or contact its operator. Removing the push gateway from an account stops push notifications for it; the gateway may keep the token until it expires.

Children

Leleka is not directed at children under 13.
